💡 Why Proportional Tax Math Matters:

If someone only ordered a ₹60 soda while someone else ate a ₹350 biryani, splitting the ₹40 GST equally is unfair. Our engine allocates tax and service charge according to each person's exact percentage of total food consumed ($S_i / S_{total}$), ensuring everyone pays their fair mathematical share.
